Mission & Vision

Building the premier kids’ ballroom competition

We are committed to the next generation of ballroom dancers, aiming to create the premier kids’ ballroom competition in the United States, and ultimately a global event.

Brilliant Dance Festival recognizes young ballroom dancer talent with professionalism and respect, positioning the event as a leading competition for aspiring professionals.

What sets us apart

How we run the festival

  1. 01

    Professional recognition

    Young dancers are treated as the future professionals they are, with respect and recognition at every stage of the event.

  2. 02

    A platform to shine

    A competition built to give rising ballroom dancers a memorable stage, with the goal of becoming the premier kids’ ballroom competition in the United States.

  3. 03

    Clear, familiar rules

    Entries follow National Dance Council of America (NDCA) rules and regulations, so dancers, parents and studios know what to expect.
