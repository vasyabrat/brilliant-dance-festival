About

The Future of Dance Starts… HERE!

At the Brilliant Dance Festival, we don’t just celebrate young dancers—we honor them as the professionals they are soon to become. Every detail of our competition is designed to recognize the passion, skill, and dedication these young performers bring to the floor. We are committed to giving them an experience that treats them with the respect and recognition they deserve, making this event truly one-of-a-kind and unforgettable.

Mission & Vision

Our mission & vision

We are driven by a commitment to the next generation of ballroom dancers, offering a platform where young competitors are treated with the same professionalism and respect as their senior counterparts. Our goal is to create a competition where these young dancers feel valued in every way that matters to them: from the supportive yet competitive environment to the generous awards and the meticulous organization for a well run and stress free event.

We aspire to make the Brilliant Dance sport Festival the premier kids’ ballroom competition in the United States, and ultimately a global event. We aim to provide a professional-grade experience that not only motivates young dancers to keep excelling but also solidifies their place in the ballroom industry.
