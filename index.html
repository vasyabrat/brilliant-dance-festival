Competition Registration Camp Information

The weekend

Two events, one festival

Train with coaches on Friday and Saturday, then compete on Sunday. Dancers can take part in either or both.

Training Camp

January 8–9, 2027

Two days of training ahead of the competition: private lessons, lectures and practice rounds. $200 Friday · $200 Saturday · $380 both days.

Camp information

Competition

January 10, 2027

A one-day competition in International Ballroom, International Latin, American Smooth and American Rhythm, for dancers from Minis through U21. Entries include solos, amateur couples and teacher/student.

Competition overview

Why Brilliant

Built around young dancers

Brilliant Dance Festival recognizes young ballroom dancer talent with professionalism and respect, positioning the event as a leading competition for aspiring professionals.

  1. 01

    Professional Recognition

    We honor young dancers with the respect they deserve, offering a professional-grade competition experience.

  2. 02

    Top-Tier Judges

    Our experienced judges provide expert feedback, ensuring fairness and high standards.

  3. 03

    Exceptional Awards

    Generous prizes celebrate the dedication and achievements of every participant.

  4. 04

    Unique Platform for Young Talent

    The festival is a unique opportunity for rising ballroom stars to shine on a national stage.
